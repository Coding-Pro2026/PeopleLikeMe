How the Vibe Engine works

No black box. Here is exactly how your match score is computed — and you can see both halves of it on every card.

The score, in one line

vibe = confidence × ( 0.6 × (0.55·P + 0.45·I) + 0.4 × P0.55·I0.45 )

  • P — Personality (55%). Your Yes / Maybe / No answers are compared question by question. Each question has a weight (deep conversations and kindness count more than night-owl vs early-bird). “Maybe” carries less information, so it counts for less. For “flex” questions like planner vs spontaneous, opposites can still click, so disagreement is penalised half as hard.
  • I — Interests (45%). A cosine similarity over everything you picked, weighted by rarity: sharing a love of classical & jazz says more than sharing a love of pizza. Related interests (anime ↔ manga) earn partial credit. The curve saturates, so ticking 30 boxes doesn’t help — honesty does. Typed favourites (“Spirited Away” ≈ “spirited away (2001)”) are fuzzy-matched for a small bonus.
  • Blend. Part arithmetic, part geometric mean — so a great match needs both a compatible temperament and common ground.
  • Confidence. If there’s little evidence (lots of “maybe”), the score is pulled toward the middle rather than overpromising.

The score is symmetric: you and the other person always see the same number.

Choosing your 10

  1. Only people inside both of your distance limits, who are verified and haven’t been blocked, passed or reported.
  2. Ranked by vibe, with a gentle boost for people closer to you.
  3. Learns your taste: waves nudge similar people up; “Not for me” and Refresh nudge them down (by at most 6 points, so a poor match can never leapfrog a great one).
  4. Diversity: a re-ranking step stops the list becoming ten near-identical people.
  5. Wildcards: up to two slots go to people whose temperament clicks with yours but whose interests differ — friendships you’d never have searched for.
  6. Refresh never repeats someone you’ve just seen. When you’ve seen everyone nearby, you get a fresh round.

Surprise Spot

For every interest you pick we know which kinds of public places fit it (sci-fi → cinema, arcade, comic shop; baking → bakery, café…). For each place type we compute how likely each of you is to enjoy it, take the geometric mean of the two, remove any place type either of you listed under “Places I like to go”, then randomly draw — weighted by that fit — and pick a real, named public venue near the midpoint using OpenStreetMap. Because it’s weighted-random, a re-roll always gives something new.

What we do not use

No photos, no looks, no income, no follower counts, no “engagement” tricks. We don’t sell your data. Your score is a tool to help you start a conversation, not a judgement of you.
