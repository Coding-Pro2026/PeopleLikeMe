What we don’t do

We don’t sell your data, show ads, use tracking cookies or analytics, or collect photos, contacts or exact GPS positions. We use a single essential, HttpOnly session cookie.

Under-18 attempts

If someone enters an under-18 birthday we store only an irreversible hash of the email to stop immediate re-tries, and nothing else.

Third parties

When you search a location or ask for a Surprise Spot, our server queries OpenStreetMap services (Nominatim and Overpass). Only the search text or a rounded coordinate is sent — never your name or email.

Your rights

In Me you can download all your data and permanently delete your account (profile, answers, chats). If you were the subject of an upheld safety report we keep an irreversible hash of your email so banned accounts cannot simply re-register.

Security

Passwords hashed with scrypt; session tokens stored only as hashes; HTTPS with HSTS in production; strict Content-Security-Policy; rate limiting and lock-outs on sign-in; all database access is parameterised.
